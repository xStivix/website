AI WORKFLOW

Generative AI

Since early 2023, I have been working with generative AI, starting with Stable Diffusion and early versions of Midjourney and continuously adapting as the technology evolved. Now generative AI has become one of the most transformative tools in video production, fundamentally changing how videos and visual effects are produced. I primarily use an image-to-video approach because it provides greater control over composition, lighting, and art direction. With carefully prepared reference material and detailed refinement, today’s tools can create near-photorealistic visuals and sequences that closely resemble traditionally produced footage. My focus is realism. I try to get as close to a realistic, authentic feel as possible when using AI.

Seedance 4K

This visualizer explores an image-to-video workflow using Seedance 4K, a model released in June 2026. With strong reference images, the model delivers exceptional visual quality and fidelity, but achieving a production-ready result requires substantial iteration. A single 15-second generation costs approximately $15–20, and the same sequence often needs to be generated three to ten times before the strongest moments can be assembled in the edit. As a result, 15 seconds of finished footage can easily exceed $250. The final piece was edited, color-corrected, and refined through a traditional post-production workflow.

May 2026

Audi Q5 Sportback

This is my personal Director’s Cut of an advertisement created for Audi Spain. It differs fundamentally from the original commercial and uses entirely AI-generated footage, with every shot extensively edited and refined. The vehicle shown is based on a specific Audi Q5 Sportback configuration. To keep it as consistent as possible across shots, I used detailed photographs and 3D renders from multiple angles. The only creative liberty I took was adding a fictional gear shifter for extra visual impact, although the Q5 does not feature one in this form. Despite the references, every image and final video still required careful re-tracking of the Audi logos, number plates, defining model details, and the shape of the light signatures. Extensive color correction and post-production helped reduce the recognizable AI look, making the finishing process especially time-consuming. The edit, post-production, and sound design were all completed through a traditional workflow.

October 2025

Sora 2 Beta Test

I created this test video with the preview version of Sora 2. As of October 1st, Sora 2 mainly supports text-to-video; image-to-video allows one reference image per generation and serves more as a visual guideline than a true starting point. For this test, I used a quick text-to-video approach, which is much faster but less visually consistent in its results. The main drawbacks are the watermarks and the 352p resolution limit, making it usable only for testing its theoretical capabilities. Despite that, its physics understanding and ability to generate multiple consistent angles within its 10-second window set a new benchmark. The full video process took about five to six hours, from the initial concept to manual music mixing and editing in After Effects. Most engine sounds came directly from Sora 2. This is not a polished cut, just a demo of what may soon be possible. The video reached over 5M views across all platforms.

December 2024

Ultimate Drive

This is my first-ever full proof of concept based on AI-generated footage using an early image-to-video workflow, similar to today’s best practices but with much less control. My aim was to show that AI-driven footage, supported by traditional pre- and post-production plus sound and music mixing, can already feel fast-paced, believable, and somewhat coherent. This clip focuses more on the results of traditional editing craftsmanship than on raw AI output alone. This is the result of over 80 hours of hands-on work: generating images and videos, editing, sound design, VFX and color grading. Images were created with Midjourney 6.1, refined in Photoshop and Magnific AI, animated with Kling 1.6 AI and Veo 2, and finalized in After Effects with flares, flames, lighting effects, camera shake, and a 3D Porsche logo. Additional upscaling was done with Topaz AI. Below is a before-and-after comparison, as well as the final clip, which generated over 2M views across all platforms.

Tech Insights

Video Editing.

My workflow is built mainly around industry-standard Adobe programs.
For AI-assisted tasks, I use a wide range of tools for image & video generation as well as editing. My main OS is Windows, but I’m also comfortable on macOS.

Color Management

I can work with all major camera systems: ARRI Alexa (Log C), RED Komodo & DSMC2 (REDCODE RAW / Log3G10), Blackmagic RAW & Sony S-Log. My pipeline is designed around Rec. 709 (Log to Rec. 709 conversion).

Programs & Tools

My main program is Adobe After Effects but I also work with Premiere, Photoshop, and DaVinci Resolve. In addition, I use a wide range of AI tools, including Midjourney, Nano Banana, Seedance, Topaz, Kling, ChatGPT, Gemini, Veo, and others.

Delivery Formats

I deliver in all common industry formats, including ProRes 422 HQ, ProRes 4444, and H.264/HEVC (up to 4K resolution). Depending on the project's needs, I also provide custom outputs for broadcast, social media platforms, and/or web use.

Learning

AI Masterclass

In this Masterclass, I share my complete AI production workflow. From prompt development and reference preparation to image generation, video creation, refinement, and final post-production integration. The course covers the latest tools, proven industry-standard best practices, and practical tips and tricks.

Workflow & Structure

The Masterclass is designed for individuals, companies, and teams with prior experience in traditional or AI filmmaking. While key fundamentals are covered where necessary, this is not a beginner step-by-step course. Across five video lessons, I break down my practical AI production workflow, using real projects for brands such as Audi and Porsche. The focus is on quality control and practical solutions to common production challenges. These methods can also be adapted to projects beyond the automotive industry.

01Shot Design & Image Creation

  • Developing shots through prompting and references
  • My image-generation workflow
  • Iterating towards a consistent visual direction
  • Editing and preparing source images while preserving detail and quality

02Video Generation

  • Structuring prompts for motion, camera behavior, and performance
  • Maintaining visual consistency and image quality

03VFX & AI Integration

  • Replacing objects within existing footage
  • Integrating AI-generated elements into existing footage
  • Adding VFX while maintaining the quality of the original image or video

04Post-Production & Upscaling

  • Upscaling and refining AI-generated footage
  • Compositing, color correction, and final polishing

05Final Thoughts & Takeaways

  • Key principles from the complete workflow
  • Practical approaches that can be applied to future projects
  • Final considerations & what to look out for in the future.

Individual Sessions

Every Masterclass includes an additional one-hour private video consultation. This session gives you the opportunity to ask follow-up questions, discuss individual challenges, and receive guidance tailored to your specific projects, workflow, or goals. We can review your current work, troubleshoot creative or technical challenges, and revisit specific parts of the course through practical examples.

Tool Disclaimer: Throughout the Masterclass, I use After Effects, Photoshop, and Topaz, alongside platforms such as Midjourney, Higgsfield, and Martini, and LLMs such as ChatGPT. The course does not cover the fundamentals of these tools; it shows how I use them within my production and post-production workflow. The principles and techniques are not tied to this specific setup and can be applied with alternatives such as Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, other AI platforms, or LLMs such as Claude.
